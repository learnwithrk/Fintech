Objectives: understand India Stack layers, digital payments, digital lending, microfinance and crowdfunding.

2.1 India Stack

India Stack is a set of open APIs enabling presence-less, paperless, cashless service delivery. It has four layers: identity (Aadhaar), payments (UPI), data (Account Aggregator / DigiLocker) and consent.

2.2 Unique Identification and eKYC

Aadhaar is a 12-digit number issued by UIDAI using biometrics and demographics. eKYC lets a bank verify identity by Aadhaar OTP or biometric, replacing physical documents.

Example: A customer opens a Demat account in about 10 minutes with Aadhaar OTP eKYC instead of submitting photocopies.

2.3 Digital Signature

eSign allows Aadhaar-authenticated signing of documents online, legally valid under the IT Act, 2000. A Digital Signature Certificate (DSC) uses public-key cryptography to ensure authenticity, integrity and non-repudiation.

2.4 Cashless Digital Payments

Modes: UPI, IMPS, NEFT, RTGS, cards, wallets (Paytm), AePS, BHIM and NETC FASTag. UPI links bank accounts through a Virtual Payment Address, enabling instant 24×7 transfers.

2.5 FinTech in Lending

Digital lenders use alternate data (mobile usage, GST returns, bank statements) to score borrowers quickly. Types: peer-to-peer (RBI-regulated NBFC-P2P), marketplace lending, invoice financing, BNPL, and salary-advance loans.

2.6 Micro Finance and Crowd Funding

Microfinance provides small loans to low-income groups, typically through Self Help Groups and MFIs (e.g., SHG–Bank Linkage). Crowdfunding raises small sums from many people: reward-based (Ketto), donation-based, equity-based and debt-based.

Example: A start-up raises ₹40 lakh from 800 small investors on an equity crowdfunding platform.

2.7 Case Study: Use of DigiLocker

DigiLocker, launched in 2015, is a government cloud wallet for authentic digital documents (Aadhaar, PAN, driving licence, mark sheets). Documents issued in it have the same legal validity as originals under IT Rules, 2016. Impact: students share certificates with employers instantly, banks fetch KYC with consent, and paper use and forgery fall. Issues: digital literacy and connectivity gaps.
